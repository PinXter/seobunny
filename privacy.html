SEOBunny – Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SEOBunny is a browser extension for checking the on-page SEO of websites. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

What we collect

Nothing. SEOBunny has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. The developer never receives any data from the extension.

What stays on your device

The following is stored locally in your browser using the standard extension storage, and never leaves your device unless you export it yourself:

  • Your settings (language, theme, highlight colors, scan options)
  • The results of your scans
  • Your AI provider API key, if you choose to add one

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Website content

When you scan a page or a site, SEOBunny reads the page content (titles, meta tags, headings, links, image alt texts) in order to show you the results. Whole-site scans fetch pages of the site you pointed the extension at. This processing happens locally in your browser.

Optional AI feature

If you connect your own API key and ask SEOBunny to draft meta content, the relevant text of the scanned page is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI — together with your API key. This happens only when you explicitly trigger it. The developer is not part of this exchange and cannot see it. The provider's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Permissions

SEOBunny requests access to websites so it can scan the pages you choose and draw the highlight overlay on them. It runs only when you use it; it does not monitor your browsing.
