SEOBunny privacy notice

Updated 1 November 2026. Covers SEOBunny 2.4.2 and its optional Pro licensing.

Who provides SEOBunny

SEOBunny is provided by BinkyCode OÜ, registry code 17575236, Sõle tn 27d-28, 10614 Tallinn, Estonia. For product support and privacy requests, email support@binkycode.com. You can also use our contact page.

Your audit data stays in your browser

SEOBunny inspects pages and sites you select. Audit results, scan history, saved comparisons, Search Console CSV data, drafts, project analyses, uploaded logos and fonts, client profiles, AI-provider API keys and license activation details are stored in the extension’s local browser storage. Language, appearance and other preferences may synchronise through your signed-in Chrome profile.

Reports and backups are created when you request them and saved to your device. We do not operate an audit-data server, receive your audit results or provider keys, sell audit data, or include developer analytics, advertising or telemetry in the extension.

Requests made while you use a tool

Audits fetch the selected website’s pages, sitemaps, robots.txt and relevant resources. Link and image checks can contact the resources being checked. Those websites receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser/network details. Where you explicitly include private pages or apply reviewed changes through a supported website integration, the relevant website receives authenticated requests. Your website credentials are not sent to BinkyCode.

Search Console priorities uses the CSV you choose to import. It does not sign in to your Google account or connect to the Search Console API.

Optional AI

If you choose an external AI provider, model discovery contacts that provider. Testing a selected model sends a small request through your API account. Generating a draft sends selected page content and metadata, your instructions and the provider’s API key directly to the provider you select. BinkyCode does not receive these requests or keys. Provider access, retention, usage limits and charges are governed by your provider account and its terms.

Local browser AI, when supported by your browser, hardware and language, processes the draft through the browser’s local model interface. The browser may first download a model. This route does not require an external AI-provider API key. Review all generated text before using it.

Optional purchases and license activation

Pro purchases, billing, invoices, renewals and customer accounts are handled on Freemius-hosted pages. Payment-card details are entered with the checkout/payment provider, not in SEOBunny. Freemius handles purchase and payment information under its privacy policy. BinkyCode can access customer, order, subscription and license information made available in its seller dashboard to provide the purchase, support and applicable business records.

When you activate a license, the extension sends Freemius the entered license key, a random per-installation identifier, the extension version and a generic installation title. Validation and device release also use the assigned installation ID. These requests contain no audited URLs, scan results, report files, uploaded branding or AI-provider keys. Freemius receives normal request/network information. Activation does not opt you into marketing.

The license key, installation identifier and ID/token, plan, paid-term expiration and last successful verification time are stored locally. The extension normally rechecks after 24 hours. A verified, unexpired license can keep Pro access without a connection for up to seven days after successful verification, ending sooner if the paid term expires. Reconnecting and validating restores eligible access. Cancelling automatic renewal preserves the remaining paid term; a revoked license no longer grants Pro access.

Support and external links

If you contact us, we process the information you send to respond, investigate the issue and provide support. Please avoid including API keys, license keys, passwords or private audit data unless specifically needed and agreed through a suitable support channel. The website contact form has its own website privacy notice.

Ko-fi, card-donation, social, help and other external links open services with their own privacy practices. Support-button artwork is bundled with the extension. A tip is optional and separate from buying Pro; tipping does not activate a license.

Retention and your controls

  • Settings > Scanned sites lets you remove saved scan data. That action preserves preferences, API keys, report assets and client profiles. Remove or reset those separately where appropriate.
  • You can remove saved client profiles, uploaded branding and API keys through their controls, or remove the extension and its local storage. Chrome-synchronised preferences are managed through your Chrome account.
  • Export backups before deleting information you want to keep. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel a paid subscription. Manage renewal through the Freemius customer portal.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request. Purchase, accounting and dispute records may need to be kept for applicable legal obligations; Freemius and other service providers apply their own retention rules.

Your privacy rights

We process purchase and support information to provide the requested service, handle correspondence and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on the processing, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, and request portability. Contact support@binkycode.com. You may complain to Estonia’s Data Protection Inspectorate or your competent supervisory authority.

Optional providers may process information outside your country under their own terms and safeguards. Choose which optional services to use and review their policies before sending confidential content. We will update this notice when the extension’s data practices change and disclose material changes through the relevant product/store surfaces.
